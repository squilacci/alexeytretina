Case studies

Selected work, from AI-powered operations tools to blockchain trading platforms.

Spensa

Spensa is an AI-powered restaurant management platform aimed at reducing running costs. Users see where the money goes and let AI surface what matters. Modern restaurants use Spensa to stay in control of everything, from ordering and daily operations to recipes and cost calculation.

Upwork

Upwork hired me to redesign their enterprise solutions features. Enterprise clients needed a new onboarding process and simplifying the process of bringing their own freelancers to the platform. As a result of work being done, the onboarding completion rate has improved by 24%.

Morpher

Over the course of three years, I worked with Morpher, a blockchain-based trading platform, to lead the design initiatives and build mobile and desktop apps. Morpher empowers everyday users to trade a wide range of assets: from stocks and crypto to luxury watches and rare collectibles.

Mana

Mana is a tool for managing schedules, events, and appointments. You can sync all your calendars inside the app and stay organized like never before. One of the main challenges was to make the app useful in a very competitive field of personal productivity and calendars.
